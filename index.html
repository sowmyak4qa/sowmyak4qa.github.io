About

I lead quality for medical device software — where a defect isn’t an inconvenience, it’s a patient safety issue.

Over nearly twelve years in engineering and quality, I’ve built a QA function from first hire to a five-person team, architected the test automation behind it, and owned the release gate for four production applications.
